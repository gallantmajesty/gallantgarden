# Focuslily Brand Foundation

## Naming Convention
**Focuslily** — one word, lowercase 'l'. Never "Focus Lily", "FocusLily", or "focuslily".

---

## Mission (Why Focuslily Exists)

Focuslily exists to help people turn good intentions into consistent action.

Many people know what they should do, but struggle to stay focused, organized, and consistent over time. Focuslily brings planning, focus sessions, progress tracking, habit building, analytics, and personal growth into one unified experience that makes meaningful progress easier to sustain.

---

## Core Problem

People juggle 5+ disconnected tools:
- Notes app
- Calendar
- Timer
- Habit tracker
- Analytics tool
- To-do list

Context switching destroys focus. Data lives in silos. Progress is invisible. Consistency breaks.

---

## Solution

One connected productivity ecosystem where every part works together:

| Connection | Result |
|------------|--------|
| Focus session → Analytics | Real-time insight into depth & consistency |
| Analytics → Recommendations | Personalized focus length, best hours, recovery needs |
| Goals → Progress tracking | Visible momentum toward what matters |
| Habits → Achievements | Compound growth, not isolated streaks |
| Calendar → Reminders | Intentions become scheduled commitments |
| Planning → Focus sessions | Structured time, not reactive chaos |

**Every action contributes to one connected system.** No tool switching. No context loss.

---

## Target Audience

Students · Professionals · Freelancers · Creators · Researchers · Developers · Designers · Remote workers · Lifelong learners

Anyone who wants to build better habits, stay focused, and see meaningful progress over time.

---

## Brand Feeling

When someone visits Focuslily, they should feel:

✅ Calm
✅ Motivated
✅ Organized
✅ Inspired
✅ In control

**NOT:**
❌ Gaming
❌ Neon
❌ Overwhelming
❌ Toy-like
❌ Childish

---

## Product Principles

Every feature must satisfy at least one:

1. **Reduce distractions**
2. **Increase clarity**
3. **Save time**
4. **Encourage consistency**
5. **Visualize progress**
6. **Reward effort**
7. **Build long-term habits**
8. **Keep users motivated**

---

## What NOT to Copy

Do not create a clone of:
- Notion
- Linear
- Apple
- Framer

Use them only as inspiration for craft quality. Focuslily must develop its own recognizable identity.

---

## Copy Standards

- Every heading memorable, every paragraph purposeful
- No generic AI phrases, no buzzwords, no excessive exclamation marks, no clichés
- Calm, confident, premium — think Stripe, Apple, Linear
- **No fake numbers, no fake testimonials, no fabricated social proof**
- Benefit-led, not feature-led

---

## Future-Proofing

Navigation and landing page must scale to support:
- Future tools & integrations
- AI capabilities
- Mobile apps
- Team workspaces
- Enterprise features

Without redesigning information architecture.

---

## The Golden Rule

**Every design decision must answer: "Does this help users understand, trust, or benefit from Focuslily?" If no → simplify or remove.**

---

## Landing Page Psychology (Section Order)

Each section answers one psychological question:

| Section | User Question |
|---------|---------------|
| Hero | "What is this?" |
| Problem | "Do I have this pain?" |
| Solution | "How does Focuslily fix it?" |
| How Focuslily Works | "What's the experience?" |
| Product Preview | "What will I actually get?" |
| Key Benefits | "Why should I care?" |
| Feature Ecosystem | "What can it do?" |
| Why It's Different | "Why not [other tools]?" |
| FAQ | "What concerns do I have?" |
| CTA | "Am I ready to start?" |

## 3D Strategy

- Enhances — never blocks — the experience
- Hero: Single lightweight Canvas (~50KB gzipped, no post-processing)
- Feature cards: CSS/WebGL hybrid, hover = micro-animation
- Auto-pause offscreen, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Load instantly, defer non-critical 3D

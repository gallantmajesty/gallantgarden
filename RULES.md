## 🔒 Identity & content

### Rule 1 — Usernames are sacred, isolated, encrypted, unique
1. **Never change anyone's username** (display name / handle) anywhere in the
   web. A username is defined by the user and shown exactly as-is. Do not
   "correct", rename, normalize, or "fix" it. (e.g. `Leadeer` stays `Leadeer`.)
2. **A username is bound to its own account only.** Every user's username must
   be linked to *their own* identity and data — **end-to-end isolated**:
   - My friend's username must never appear in my session, UI, or data.
   - My username must never appear in my friend's session, UI, or data.
   - No account's identity may leak into another account (no cross-contamination
     between sessions, profiles, tasks, or any view).
3. **End-to-end username encryption.** Usernames are treated as private
   identity and must remain encrypted end-to-end — never stored or transmitted
   in a way that exposes one user's name to another. Keep isolation at the data
   boundary; do not surface another user's username to a different user.
4. **Player ID is globally unique for all.** Every user gets exactly one
   Player ID, unique across the entire system. Never reuse, collide, or share a
   Player ID between users.

- Do not modify user-authored content (usernames, bios, task text, notes)
  unless explicitly asked.

## 🤖 AI / honesty

### Rule 2 — No fake AI, ever
1. **This web has NO AI integration.** Do not present, claim, label, or imply
   that any UI content is generated by AI. There is no AI behind the app, so
   nothing should be dressed up as an "AI" feature.
2. **Never add fake-AI surfaces** — no "AI summary", "AI insight", "AI coach",
   "AI ribbon", or similar, and no "AI" badges/labels on content that is just
   static copy, heuristics, or deterministic logic. (The existing insight
   panels are product copy, not AI — do not rename or extend them as "AI".)
3. **No fabricated information.** Do not invent stats, summaries, or messages
   and attribute them to intelligence the app does not have. Show only real
   data or clearly-static, honest copy.
4. When in doubt, leave it out: prefer a clean, honest, minimal UI over any
   feature that pretends to be smarter than it is.

## 🎨 UI / design
- **Keep it minimal.** Calm, restrained, lots of whitespace, clear hierarchy,
  few elements. No clutter, no feature-piling.
- **Research before building.** Use the web (best-practice articles, design
  galleries, real product references) to find the best possible minimal
  dashboard / HQ design, then apply that — don't guess.

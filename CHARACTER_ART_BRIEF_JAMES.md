# Character Art Brief — JAMES (v3 — Magical School Boy)

## Style

**Semi-realistic anime / Genshin Impact style** — detailed illustration, clean line art, soft shading, fabric folds, hair strands, subtle lighting. Magical school aesthetic. NOT flat cartoon. Think **Harry Potter meets Genshin Impact** — academy student with magical flair.

**Name:** James

**Age:** 17-18

## Outfit — Magical Academy Uniform

### Outer Robe (Floor-length, worn open)
- **Color:** Navy blue (#1B2B5A) with gold (#D4AF37) constellation trim
- **Style:** Long academic robe, open front, reaches mid-shin
- **Details:**
  - High collar that stands up behind neck
  - Gold star and moon pattern along hem and collar edges
  - Purple (#5A2A5A) lining visible where robe falls open
  - Gold clasp at chest (circular, with crescent moon emblem)
  - Robe flows naturally with fabric weight — not stiff
  - Subtle magical shimmer on gold trim (not glowing, just luminous)

### Jacket (Under robe)
- **Color:** Deep midnight blue (#1A1A2E)
- **Style:** Slim-fit academy blazer
- **Details:**
  - Gold buttons (3 visible on front)
  - Academy crest on left chest (small, detailed)
  - Ribbed cuffs with gold stripe
  - Shoulder seams, back vent
  - Visible under open robe

### Shirt
- **Color:** Warm cream (#E8E0D4)
- **Style:** Button-up dress shirt
- **Details:**
  - Collar visible above jacket
  - Top button undone (relaxed)
  - Slight wrinkle texture
  - Tucked into pants

### Pants
- **Color:** Dark navy (#2A2A3E)
- **Style:** Slim-fit dress pants
- **Details:**
  - Crease line down front of each leg
  - Belt loops with brown leather belt
  - Slight fabric stacking at ankles

### Belt
- **Color:** Brown leather (#4A2E1A)
- **Buckle:** Gold (#D4AF37), circular with crescent moon emblem
- **Position:** At waist, slightly left of center

### Shoes
- **Color:** White cream (#E9E6DF)
- **Style:** Clean leather dress shoes (not sneakers)
- **Details:**
  - Rounded toe, subtle sole line
  - Slight shine/highlight on toe cap

### Magical Accessory
- **Wand holster:** Small brown leather strap on inner left forearm (visible when arms at sides)
- **No wand visible** — just the holster. Wand is hidden/in pocket.
